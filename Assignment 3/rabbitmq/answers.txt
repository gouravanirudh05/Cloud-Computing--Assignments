2. While the selected consumer was stopped, did the other consumer continue to receive messages? Answer Yes/No.
Ans:- Yes

3. After restarting the stopped consumer, did it receive the messages that were sent while it was stopped? Answer Yes/No and briefly explain why.
Ans:- No. vowel_consumer.py uses a temporary exclusive queue. When the consumer stopped, its queue was deleted, so messages published to it while it was stopped were not stored. Restarting the consumer created a new queue for it.

4. After restarting the stopped consumer, run the producer again. Did both consumers receive the new messages? Answer Yes/No and briefly explain your observation.
Ans:- Yes,Both consumers had  queues that were actively bound to the fanout exchange,so the new messages was delivered to both consumers.
